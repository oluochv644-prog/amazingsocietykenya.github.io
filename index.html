Our Purpose

We are committed to serving communities with compassion, integrity, accountability and respect for human dignity.

Creating opportunities for people and communities to thrive.
Who We Are

Building stronger, empowered and dignified communities.

AMAZING SOCIETY is a non-profit, non-political and non-partisan community-based organization established to serve and empower communities.

Our Vision

To see transformed, empowered and dignified communities where young people and vulnerable persons have opportunities to thrive and reach their full potential.

Our Mission

To transform communities through youth empowerment, mentorship, Christian values, dignity support, health education, elderly care, career development and community service.

Our Programs

Where we make a difference

Our programs respond to community needs while creating opportunities for growth, dignity, service and positive transformation.

👥

Youth Empowerment

Mentorship, life skills, leadership development, talent development and community engagement.

🎓

Career Empowerment

Career guidance, professional mentorship, career talks, networking, entrepreneurship and employability support.

🩺

Health & SRH Mentorship

Responsible mentorship on sexual and reproductive health, personal safety, healthy relationships and appropriate health information.

🩷

Menstrual Hygiene & Dignity

Menstrual hygiene education, sanitary support, dignity kits and mentorship aimed at reducing menstrual stigma.

👵

Elderly & Community Care

Visits, companionship, outreach and appropriate material, social and emotional support for elderly persons in need.

✝️

Christian Mentorship

Mentorship founded on love, compassion, integrity, forgiveness, service, responsibility and respect.

🤝

Community Transformation

Community needs assessment, volunteerism, social responsibility, resource mobilization and community service.
